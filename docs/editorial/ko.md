# Editorial notes: Korean (ko)

Edited with the `blog-editor-ko` skill (with `blog-editor-core/REFERENCE.md`, including the owner-confirmed facts in section 11). No `MASTHEAD.md` existed for ko during this pass, so section 10's masthead pass was not run.

**Desk-wide consistency pass (all 10 articles).** Two writers had produced the drafts: one wrote best-apps, polarsteps, offline, google-timeline and cherry-blossom; the other wrote countries, how-to, places-vs-moments, print and prompts, and their prose read more like translated English. Changes applied across the whole desk:
- Register: 해요체 everywhere. No 합니다체 or 반말 sentences were found in the prose. In how-to, the H3s written in 한다체 (“그 자리에 있을 때 쓴다”) became noun phrases (“그 자리에서 쓰기”). Quoted traveller notes keep their 음슴체 because that is how people actually write notes.
- Quotation marks: every straight "…" in the body and inside frontmatter values is now “ ”.
- Brand forms: 폴라스텝스 everywhere, with “폴라스텝스(Polarsteps)” at the first body mention. Before, best-apps and offline used only Polarsteps, and the polarsteps article switched between the two forms. Internal-link anchors now match the target titles: “폴라스텝스 대안” and “구글 지도 타임라인 대체” (they had been “Polarsteps 대안” and “구글 지도 타임라인 대안”).
- Maps and Google in prose: 구글 지도, 애플 지도, 구글 계정, 구글 서버. Before, the drafts used Google 지도, Apple 지도, Google 계정 and Google 서버.
- Map links: every “지도 링크를 붙여 넣으면” now says 애플 지도나 구글 지도 링크, so readers do not assume Naver or Kakao links work.
- Moments and ratings: several sentences presented “no rating on moments” as an app rule, or said moments have no location. en.js has every field except the title optional. These now read as advice (“별점을 비워 두세요”), and the claims “순간에는 위치와 별점이 둘 다 없어요” and “장소에만 5점 만점” are corrected.
- Owner facts (section 11): where an article puts the trip map next to offline use, it now says the offline map shows only saved places and is not for finding your way (offline, polarsteps). PDF descriptions now say a PDF can run to several pages and is not a photo-book layout (print, offline).
- Spacing: 가 본 나라 everywhere except the searched form in the countries title, H1, lede and FAQ questions. 오래가요. “…”처럼 with no space before 처럼.
- Smell list: 꿀팁 now appears at most once per article; dropped -들 plurals (앱들이, 질문들은, 사람들의, 것들이요); replaced 가지다 calques; removed “~인 셈이에요” on plain facts; removed unverifiable generalisations (“찾는 사람이 늘었어요”, “대부분이 결국 고르는 기준”, “서른 개국 언저리에서 흥미를 잃어요”).
- Disclosure (“저희가 만드는 앱”): kept in every article, and added to cherry-blossom, which named VoyageLog features without saying we make it.

**Fact-check note.** The web search budget ran out late in the pass (searches are shared across the session). A KakaoTalk photo-compression claim and the Fukuoka/Tokyo bloom-order claim could not be checked, so both were generalised or removed (see the articles).
