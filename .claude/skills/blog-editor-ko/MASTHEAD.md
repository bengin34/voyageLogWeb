# Masthead lens: Korean market

The quality bar for the Korean desk, taken from how the most trusted Korean travel publishers and the national consumer-testing body check their own work. It is a set of standards, never a source of content (reference, section 10). Everything below is paraphrased; quotes are a few words at most.

**Research status (2026-10-24): provisional.** The session's web search and fetch budgets ran out before any page could be read, so **nothing below was verified today**. Each point carries one of these labels:
- **Known, re-check:** a published policy or rule we know exists from earlier knowledge. The URL is where to confirm it. Before you rely on a detail, read the page.
- **Inferred:** read from the publication's published work as we know it, not from a written policy.

The Editor's lens questions (below) do not depend on these details: they come from our house rules and the general standards, and they apply as they stand. When the web tools are available again, re-run the research and replace each "Known, re-check" label with "Stated" plus the exact page.

## 1. 한겨레 ESC and the 여행 desk (hani.co.kr): the newspaper benchmark

**Why it is the benchmark.** `SKILL.md` already names 한겨레 ESC as the tone standard. A national daily's weekend lifestyle and travel section is where Korean readers expect practical travel writing that is trustworthy, as opposed to the 체험단 posts that fill Naver results. A newspaper desk also works under a written code and a corrections practice, which a blog does not.

**Editorial QC, in our words**
- **Known, re-check:** 한겨레 has published a 윤리강령 since its founding (1988) and later a more detailed 취재보도준칙. Both cover accepting gifts, hospitality and paid trips from sources, and keeping advertising apart from editorial. Re-check at the company pages linked from https://www.hani.co.kr (회사소개, 윤리강령 / 취재보도준칙).
- **Known, re-check:** the paper prints corrections under a standing heading (바로잡습니다) and has a reader-side review body (열린편집위원회 / 시민편집인 at various times). The point for us: an error is corrected openly and on the record, not quietly.
- **Known, re-check:** as a member of 한국신문윤리위원회 it is bound by the 신문윤리강령 and 신문윤리실천요강, which require a visible separation of 기사 and 광고 and forbid presenting advertising as reporting (기사형 광고). Re-check at https://www.ikpec.or.kr.
- **Inferred (from ESC travel pieces):** a practical box or paragraph at the end gathers the 가는 길, 운영 시간, 요금 and the season, and those details are the ones most likely to be marked as of a date or "변동 가능". A recommendation comes with a reason a reader can check (a dish, a view, a walking time), not with an adjective. When a trip was paid for by a tourism board or airline, a 취재 지원 / 취재협조 line says so.
- **Not verified:** whether 한겨레 has published a policy on generative AI in reporting.

## 2. 트래비 Travie (travie.com): the travel-magazine benchmark

**Why it is the benchmark.** A long-running Korean travel magazine (print and web) from the publisher of the travel trade paper 여행신문, so its editors know the industry and its incentives from the inside. It is the closest Korean equivalent of a dedicated travel title written for ordinary travellers, covering both domestic trips and the short outbound trips Koreans take most (일본, 동남아).

**Editorial QC, in our words**
- **Not verified:** whether Travie publishes a standalone 편집 원칙 or 윤리강령. Check the footer of https://www.travie.com (회사소개, 청소년보호정책, 광고 문의) when tools return. Registered internet newspapers in Korea must publish a 청소년보호정책 and a responsible person; that is not an editorial standard.
- **Inferred (from published work):** features end with a byline (글·사진 + reporter name) and a 취재협조 line naming the tourism board, airline or hotel that supported the trip. Sponsorship is disclosed by naming the supporter, not hidden. That is the habit we copy in spirit: our disclosure (저희가 만드는 앱이에요) sits near the top and says plainly where our interest lies.
- **Inferred:** service pieces separate the story from the information block (교통, 숙소, 요금, 운영 기간), and seasonal pieces give a month or "매년 달라짐" rather than promise a fixed date.
- **Inferred:** ranked lists are rare; selections are presented as an editor's picks with a reason each, and themed collections ("○○에 가기 좋은 곳") explain the theme before the list.

## 3. 한국소비자원 비교공감 (kca.go.kr / consumer.go.kr): the product-review benchmark

**Why it is the benchmark.** The Korea Consumer Agency's comparative test reports (비교공감, now published through the 소비자24 portal) are the national reference for "which one should I buy" in Korea. Their method is the model for our app-comparison articles, even though we do not run tests.

**Editorial QC, in our words**
- **Known, re-check:** each report names the products compared, the criteria (품질, 안전성, 경제성, 표시 사항 and so on) and how each was tested, and gives the results item by item rather than as one overall ranking. Re-check at https://www.consumer.go.kr (비교공감 / 비교정보) and https://www.kca.go.kr.
- **Known, re-check:** samples are bought on the open market, and the report says the results apply to the tested samples and the test period. Prices are stated as of the survey date.
- **Inferred:** manufacturers are told the results before publication and can respond; the report notes where a maker has improved a product after the test.
- **Not verified:** a published policy on AI use or a corrections procedure for these reports.

## 4. The disclosure baseline: 공정거래위원회 추천·보증 심사지침

Not a publication but the rule every Korean reader has learned to look for since the 뒷광고 controversy of 2020. It shapes what Korean readers read as honest.

- **Known, re-check:** the 추천·보증 등에 관한 표시·광고 심사지침 (revised with effect from 2020-09-01) requires anyone with an economic interest in a product to disclose it clearly, in Korean, where the reader sees it first (the title or the opening of the post, not hidden behind "더보기" or at the bottom), with plain wording such as 광고, 협찬 or the nature of the interest. Vague formulas and English-only tags do not count. Re-check at https://www.law.go.kr (search the title under 행정규칙) and https://www.ftc.go.kr.
- **Known, re-check:** a 추천·보증 must reflect the endorser's genuine experience and opinion; presenting a product as used when it was not is deceptive. For us this means: no "써 보니", "직접 사용해 본" or "여행에서 테스트해 보니" about any app, including our own, unless it is literally true.
- Our case: we are the maker, not an endorser, so the disclosure is the one sentence 저희가 만드는 앱이에요, in the first or second paragraph of every comparison and alternatives article. That satisfies the spirit of the rule; it does not make the article an ad, and nothing after it should read like one.

## Editor's lens: the questions to ask of every Korean article

Ask these after the line edit, in one pass. Each one comes from a standard above, adapted to what we can honestly do.

1. **협찬 test (공정위).** Is 저희가 만드는 앱이에요 (or an equally plain sentence) in the first or second paragraph of every comparison or alternatives piece, in Korean, not buried at the end? Would a reader trained by 뒷광고 coverage find it without scrolling?
2. **실제 사용 경험.** Is there any sentence claiming we used, tested or travelled with an app (써 보니, 직접 사용해 본, 한 달 동안 써 봤어요)? If it is not literally true, it goes. Say what we did: read each app's own pages and its Korean store listing.
3. **기준일.** Would every competitor fact still be true if a reader opened the app's Korean store page (`apps.apple.com/kr`, Google Play `hl=ko&gl=KR`) today? If you did not check it today, it goes or turns generic. If a fact changed, is `updated` today?
4. **원 and the right storefront.** Is every price in 원 from the Korean storefront, checked today? Otherwise, is it "무료, 유료 플랜 있음" instead of a number (비교공감 states prices as of a survey date; we cannot keep a date fresh, so we drop the number)?
5. **Criteria before verdicts (비교공감).** In a comparison, are the criteria stated before the apps (회원가입 필요 여부, 오프라인, 내보내기, 가격 방식, 지원 기기, 한국어 화면), and is every app judged on the same ones?
6. **Item-by-item, not a crown.** Does the piece let readers choose by need ("경로가 저절로 남길 원한다면") instead of naming one 최고의 앱? A single winner needs a reason the reader can check.
7. **Justified selection.** Does each app in a list carry one concrete reason it is there and one honest limit? An entry with only praise reads like a 체험단 post. Would the list still make sense if VoyageLog were not in it?
8. **Stated limits of the comparison.** Does the article say what it does not cover (prices that change, features we could not confirm, iPad or web versions not compared)?
9. **VoyageLog's own limits.** Is the "VoyageLog가 맞지 않는 경우" section specific (자동 경로 기록, 여행 계획, 클라우드 동기화, 아이패드·웹) so that a reader who needs those leaves with a better answer?
10. **The Naver/Kakao trap.** Does any sentence imply that 네이버 지도 or 카카오맵 links, 카카오톡 or Instagram integration, or Naver blog import work in VoyageLog? Only pasted Apple and Google Maps links are claimed.
11. **Practical information dated by nature.** 벚꽃 개화, 축제 기간, 운영 시간, 입장료, 웨이팅: is each verified on the official source for this year, or written as a month, a range or "매년 달라져요, 출발 전 공식 안내 확인"?
12. **Information block discipline.** Where the article gives practical details, are they gathered in one place in a consistent order instead of scattered through the prose, so a reader can re-check them before leaving?
13. **Market fit.** Is every app, service or print shop named available and relevant to a Korean reader (한국어 화면 per the store's language list, Korean storefront, delivery to Korea)?
14. **Context a store page cannot give.** Does the article add something the apps' own pages do not: how to choose, what to write, a real note from 제주, 경주, 오사카 or 다낭 (“웨이팅 40분, 2시 전에 가기”)?
15. **Sourcing without citations.** Is every verified fact stated plainly, with no 출처, "~에 따르면" or links in the text, and the source URL only in the editorial report? Is any vendor promise we cannot test framed as the vendor's own claim ("Polarsteps는 ~라고 설명해요")?
16. **Re-check the previous edition.** If the article was edited before, did you re-verify the facts that were kept, not only the new ones?
17. **Correction readiness (바로잡습니다).** If a reader wrote in that a fact is wrong, could the report for this article show where each fact was checked and when? If not, the fact is not ready to publish.
18. **Plural voice.** Is the voice 저희 throughout, with no single 에디터 or 기자 persona, no invented credentials and no 글·사진 style byline?

## What we never borrow

- Their text, headlines, 부제, list titles or section headings, in Korean or translated.
- Their rankings, list selections, picks, award labels, test grades or 우수·보통 ratings.
- Their signature formats: newspaper information boxes as laid out by them, magazine 취재협조 and 글·사진 lines, 비교공감 result tables and grading symbols.
- Recognisable phrasing, series names or slogans.
- Their names: never mention, quote, compare ourselves to or link 한겨레, ESC, 트래비, 한국소비자원, 비교공감 or any other benchmark inside an article.
- Claims we cannot make: on-the-ground reporting, a sponsored trip, lab testing, a named reporter.
