Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

VoyageLog is designed to keep your travel history private. The app works offline-first and does not require an account.

What data is stored

We do not store your travel data on our servers. Trips, notes, places, photos, tags, and related travel records are stored locally on your device by default.

Where your data lives

Your data is stored locally on your device. We do not copy your travel history to a VoyageLog server.

Exporting your data

You can export trips to PDF or CSV for your own backups or sharing. If you share an exported file, you control where it goes.

No tracking by default

VoyageLog does not include analytics or advertising trackers by default.

Crash reports

We do not collect crash reports by default.

Deleting your data

Because your data lives locally on your device, you can remove it by deleting records in the app, clearing app data, or uninstalling the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@qrodesk.com.
